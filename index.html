About Me

Hi, I'm Tega Igho

I go by KapturbyT, and what I do is simple — I tell your story through a lens. I'm a photographer and videographer based in Warri, Delta State, and I've been doing this long enough to know that the best shots aren't always planned. They're felt.

What started as a love for capturing moments on my phone grew into something I'm genuinely passionate about. I shoot events, portraits, birthdays, brand content and everything in between. My goal is always the same — make sure you look back at the content and feel something real.

I believe every person, every brand, every moment has a story worth telling beautifully. That's what I show up to do, every single time.
